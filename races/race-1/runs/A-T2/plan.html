One decision

A question that starts with = could run as a formula in a spreadsheet. Leave it as the ticket says?

The ticket says the question text goes in "as is". Spreadsheet apps treat a cell starting with =, +, - or @ as a formula, so a student could type a question that does something odd when the teacher opens the file. This is a known CSV issue, not something the ticket mentions.
