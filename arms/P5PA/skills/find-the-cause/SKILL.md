---
name: find-the-cause
description: Use on a bug ticket, when a test fails unexpectedly, or after a fix attempt has failed.
---
Reproduce the problem first and keep the command. Read the whole error. Change one thing at a time
and re-run. Fix the first place things go wrong, not the symptom.

After two failed fixes built on the same idea, write the idea down and test it directly before
trying a third.
