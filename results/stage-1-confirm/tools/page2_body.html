The stage 2 plan

P1 Loose only fails where it does not question the request. Stage 2 tests what could fix that, and whether it holds up on long work.

  • Tickets (8): the wrong-ask ticket T6 and the two new wrong-ask tickets; the two new long, many-file tickets; and T2, T5 and T7 from the current set, to catch a way of working that makes ordinary tickets worse or dearer.
  • Runs: two of every cell. Five additions on eight tickets is 80 runs, plus 8 for P1 on the four new tickets, 88 in all. P1's existing runs cover the rest.
  • Cost: about 20M tokens with grading, roughly three hours of running. The new tickets' size is still being measured, so this can move.
  • How an addition gets in: the charter's rule, unchanged. It must improve at least two of trustworthy rate, quality score and rescues, without adding more than 30% to cost. Both readings will be shown; the current rules decide.
  • Skill use is recorded. Every run logs which skills it used, so a tie between the skills versions and P1 can be read: either the skills were used and did not help, or they were never picked up.
