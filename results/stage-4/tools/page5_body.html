What I can and cannot say

  • Can: on short, well-specified tickets, with this guide and these two skills, the cheaper model is as trustworthy as the dearer one, on your app and on two projects it had never seen.
  • Cannot: that this holds for long or sprawling work. Runs here averaged 11 to 18 minutes. All three setups are near the top of the test, and a test that everyone nearly passes cannot rank them.
  • Cannot: whether a lead left to its own judgement would delegate well. Left alone it did everything itself; here it was told to hand the building over.
  • Thin: two runs per ticket per setup. On one project, half a ticket in eight is a single run.
  • Not ruled out: that a model remembered a public fix from training. The replays all post-date July 2026, and Opus and Fable did equally well on them.
